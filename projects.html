The Kitchen Story App

Oct 2023 – Mar 2024 · Patent Awarded (April 2024)

A cross-platform recipe management application designed for both web and Android users. The app allows users to organize recipes, mark favorites, and share their creations.

  • Developed using XAMPP, Android Studio, and Visual Studio.
  • Implemented features for favorites, recipe categorization, and user sharing.
  • Optimized for performance and ease of use for home cooks.
  • Recognized with a patent for innovation in digital culinary experiences.
